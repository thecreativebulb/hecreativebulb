Professional visual content for property and design.

The Creative Bulb produces real estate films for property launches, premium residences, commercial spaces and developer campaigns. We combine planned movement, composition, pacing and aerial footage where appropriate to create films for websites, presentations, YouTube and social media.

Our work is designed to give marketing and design teams a library of strong visual assets rather than a handful of disconnected images. We plan the shoot around the project, audience and final uses so the same production can support a website, brochure, listing, presentation and social campaign.

What we can deliver

  • Property walkthrough and launch films
  • Developer and project brand films
  • Vertical social reels and short-form edits
  • Aerial footage integrated into cinematic edits
