What to confirm before a drone shoot

A project-team checklist for planning aerial photography and video responsibly.

Aerial visuals can show site context, access, landscape and the relationship between a building and its surroundings. But drone work depends on location, airspace, weather, operator qualifications and the permissions or authorisations applicable to that operation.

Plan the shoot around the brief

Before booking, confirm who is operating the drone, whether the exact location and flight plan are permitted, what approvals are required, and whether the site has any security or privacy restrictions. Rules and airspace status can change, so verify requirements through current official channels for the specific date and location. Never assume that a drone flight is allowed simply because another project nearby has been filmed.

Make the images work harder

Include aerial deliverables in the initial brief and allow time for checks and approvals. If a flight is not permitted or conditions are unsafe, plan an alternative ground-based shot list.
