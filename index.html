About

A studio built around light, precision and story.

The Creative Bulb started with a simple frustration: most construction and real estate photography looks like documentation, not marketing. We build every shoot — photo, film or drone — the way a production house would, with a plan, a shot list and a point of view.

We work directly with developers, architects, interior studios and marketing teams who need visual assets that hold up on a hoarding, a listing portal and a reel in the same week.

01
PrecisionEvery shot is planned against the brief before we arrive on site.
02
